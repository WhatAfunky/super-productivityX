Auto screenshots — mockup (v3.3)

Pick an app window for a layer. While you focus on that layer (▶), the app captures that window every 5 minutes into the layer's history — a timelapse of how the work grew. Desktop app only. Buttons and the timeline are clickable.

4 · Keeping the history in check — 100 MB per layer

A capture is ~0.3–0.5 MB after WebP compression (~5 MB per hour of focus), so 100 MB ≈ 20 hours of focus on one layer. Nothing is deleted on its own.

When a layer passes 100 MB
"Storyboard · Shot A" has 104 MB of captures.
Captures keep going. What should happen to the history?
Thin out: keep every capture of the last 7 days, one per hour of focus before that, one per day after 30 days (frees most of it, keeps the whole story). Export & clear: save all as a ZIP, then delete them. Keep everything: ask again at the next 100 MB (200, 300…).

5 · Decided (2026-10-22)

  • A plain window picker (thumbnails + titles of open windows).
  • Captures every 5 min while you focus on that layer (▶), plus Capture now.
  • Each capture automatically becomes the layer's screenshot.
  • History per layer; a warning with options at 100 MB per layer (then 200…).
  • Desktop only; captures and the window choice stay on this computer (not synced).
  • Finding the window again: its window id, else a window whose title matches, else skip the capture and ask to re-pick.
